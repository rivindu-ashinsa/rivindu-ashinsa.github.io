Cognivus Labs End-to-End Digital Health Monitoring Platform

Cognivus Labs is a production-grade, real-time healthcare intelligence platform connecting wearable IoT sensing, clinical-grade backend services, and AI-driven decision support into one end-to-end ecosystem.

  • Streams ECG and SpO2 data from ESP32-based wearable patches through secure WebSocket/REST pipelines.
  • Delivers live monitoring interfaces for doctors, staff, and administrators across web and mobile.
  • Applies ML for ECG abnormality detection, SpO2 deterioration forecasting, and explainable multi-biomarker risk scoring.
  • Led and owned the full AI/ML track: research, preprocessing pipelines, inference architecture, backend integration, optimization, and production-ready clinical outputs.
